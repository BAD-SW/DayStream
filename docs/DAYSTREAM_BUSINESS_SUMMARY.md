# DayStream — Business Plan Summary

## Overview

DayStream is a multi-tenant software platform for booking and running service-based
businesses, purpose-built for the wellness, recovery, fitness, and health-optimization
market — recovery centers, spas, yoga and fitness studios, and clinics — while remaining
configurable for any appointment- and membership-driven business. It gives an operator
everything needed to sell services, take bookings, manage members and staff, process
payments, market to customers, and understand performance, all from a single system.

DayStream sits in the same category as Mindbody, Momence, WellnessLiving, Glofox, Mariana
Tek, and bsport, but is differentiated in two important ways. First, it is designed around
the premium wellness and recovery experience rather than as a generic studio tool. Second,
it is franchise- and territory-aware: a single organization can operate many business
locations under one account, and the platform is built to help an organization grow its
footprint across defined territories.

## Who it serves

DayStream is organized around four kinds of users, each with a clear scope of
responsibility:

- **Platform operators** who run DayStream itself and onboard new organizations.
- **Organization owners** who oversee all of their business locations, see performance
  across them, and use the platform's tools to expand into new territories.
- **Business teams** — owners, managers, reception, therapists, and trainers — who run the
  day-to-day of a single location.
- **Customers** who browse and book services, manage their memberships, and pay, through
  the web or a mobile app.

Users with broader responsibility can look into a specific location's view without changing
who they are, so an organization owner or platform operator can support any business
directly.

## What it does

**Booking and scheduling.** DayStream handles individual appointments, shared sessions,
group classes, and bookable rooms and equipment. Operators configure lead times, cut-off
windows, capacity, waitlists, and cancellation and reschedule policies. Staff and managers
work from day, week, month, timeline, and resource calendar views, with scheduling that is
aware of real capacity.

**Services and resources.** Businesses define their service catalog — categories, services,
and bookable variants with their own durations and prices — along with the staff qualified
to deliver them, the rooms or equipment required, tax treatment, and cancellation rules.
Rooms and equipment are modeled as managed resources with their own schedules and
double-booking protection, which matters for recovery and wellness settings where saunas,
cold baths, float rooms, and treatment rooms are the constraint.

**Staff.** Every staff member is both a user of the system and an HR record, with
qualifications, certifications, languages, availability patterns, leave, and the services
and locations they are assigned to. Permissions are role-based, from owner down to reception
and practitioner.

**Memberships, packages, and pricing.** DayStream supports unlimited memberships, credit
plans, hybrid plans, punch cards, and introductory packages, with auto-renewal, freezing,
pausing, and upgrades. Its pricing engine covers fixed, membership, promotional, seasonal,
first-time, and corporate pricing, along with discount codes.

**Payments and commerce.** The platform takes one-time and recurring payments, handles
refunds, gift cards, and stored payment methods, and supports retail alongside services. It
includes invoicing and accounts receivable, and tracks payables and payroll, with billing
that rolls up from a business to its organization.

**Customer relationships and marketing.** A full CRM stores customer profiles, booking and
attendance history, financial history, and wellness notes, and tracks each customer through
a lifecycle from lead to active member to re-engagement. Operators segment their audience
and run email and SMS campaigns and automated sequences — welcome journeys, renewal
reminders, re-engagement, and birthday offers. A community layer adds social feeds,
challenges, and leaderboards to keep members engaged.

**Growth tools.** Beyond running the business, DayStream helps organizations grow. It can
define a geographic territory and generate a prospect list of potential businesses within
it, giving an organization owner a running start on expansion rather than a blank page.

**Presentation and reach.** Businesses can run their public website through a built-in
content system with pages, blog, media, and search-engine optimization, and can embed a
booking widget directly into an existing website. A consumer mobile app lets customers
browse, book, pay, and receive reminders and notifications.

**Insight.** Reporting and analytics cover revenue over time, membership health including
churn and renewals, booking utilization, attendance and no-shows, staff productivity, and
resource utilization — the operating numbers a wellness business needs to manage capacity
and retention.

## How it stands apart

DayStream competes on the operational essentials that any serious platform in this space
must have — booking, memberships, CRM, payments, staff and resource management, reporting,
marketing, a website, and a mobile app. Where it is designed to stand apart is in its fit
for premium wellness and recovery operators, its native support for organizations that run
and grow multiple locations across territories, a refined, brand-forward customer
experience, and tooling that makes it straightforward to bring an existing business onto the
platform.
