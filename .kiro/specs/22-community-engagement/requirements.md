# Phase 22: Community & Engagement

## Status: 🔲 Not Started

## Objective
Build social and gamification features to increase customer retention and engagement.

## Dependencies
- Phase 05: Customer Management
- Phase 19: Mobile Application

## Scope Summary
- Community feed (social timeline within app)
- Challenges and programs:
  - Time-based challenges (30-day cold therapy challenge)
  - Achievement tracking
  - Leaderboards
- Gamification:
  - Points/rewards system
  - Badges and milestones
  - Streaks (consecutive visit rewards)
- Video-on-demand (VOD):
  - Wellness content library
  - Guided sessions
  - Video hosting and streaming
- Online courses / LMS:
  - Course creation
  - Progress tracking
  - Certification
- Social features:
  - Customer-to-customer messaging
  - Reviews and testimonials
  - Referral programs

## Key Decisions Pending
- Video hosting approach (self-hosted vs. Mux/Cloudflare Stream)
- LMS complexity (simple progress tracking vs. full course builder)
- Community moderation tooling
- Referral reward structure
