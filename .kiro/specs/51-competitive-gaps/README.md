## Overview

A side-by-side comparison of DayStream with MangoMint and Momence (`docs/COMPETITIVE_COMPARISON_MANGOMINT_MOMENCE.md`) found areas where DayStream leads (franchise and territory layer, prospect generation, website CMS, community, operator tooling, flat pricing) and areas where it trails. This phase collects the trailing areas in one place and works through them until each is closed to the owner's satisfaction.

Each gap is a requirement here. Gaps that already have their own spec (AI in 21, multi-language in 44, two-way messaging in 47) are tracked here and finished in that spec, so nothing is specified twice. Every gap starts by confirming its current state in the code, because the comparison is a snapshot and parts of DayStream keep moving.

**Core Features**:
- Forms, waivers and e-signature; treatment charting
- Two-way SMS and WhatsApp, text-a-link booking and payment, virtual waiting room
- Class scheduling depth, retail inventory, staff mobile app
- Video hosting and livestream, payroll payout, multi-language, AI assistant, telephony
