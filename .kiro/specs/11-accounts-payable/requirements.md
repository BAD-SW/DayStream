# Phase 11: Accounts Payable

## Status: 🔲 Not Started

## Objective
Build a reusable financial module for payroll tracking, vendor payments, and financial reporting — designed as a cornerstone component for use across future applications.

## Dependencies
- Phase 10: Payment Platform
- Phase 12: Staff Management

## Scope Summary
- Staff payroll tracking (hours, sessions, commissions)
- Pay period management
- Payroll calculation (hourly, per-session, commission-based)
- Vendor/supplier management
- Expense tracking and categorization
- Bill management and payment scheduling
- Financial reporting:
  - Profit & Loss
  - Revenue by service/category
  - Staff cost analysis
  - Expense reports
- Chart of accounts
- Journal entries
- Bank reconciliation foundations
- Multi-currency financial records
- Tax reporting support
- Export (CSV, PDF)

## Key Decisions Pending
- Scope boundary: payroll tracking vs. actual payroll processing (tax withholding, direct deposit)
- Accounting standard (single vs. double-entry bookkeeping)
- Integration with external accounting software (Xero, QuickBooks)
- Reusability architecture (standalone package vs. platform module)
