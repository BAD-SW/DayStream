# Phase 09: Pricing Engine

## Status: 🔲 Not Started

## Objective
Build a flexible pricing system supporting dynamic pricing, promotions, tiered rates, and multi-currency.

## Dependencies
- Phase 06: Service Management
- Phase 08: Membership Engine

## Scope Summary
- Fixed pricing per service (with duration variants: 30/60/90 min)
- Membership pricing (discounted rates for members)
- Promotional/sale pricing (time-limited)
- Seasonal pricing (peak/off-peak)
- First-time customer pricing
- Corporate/B2B pricing
- Volume discounts
- Bundle pricing (package deals)
- Multi-currency support
- Tax calculation and rules per jurisdiction
- Price history and audit trail
- Coupon/discount code system

## Key Decisions Pending
- Pricing rule priority/precedence logic
- Tax calculation approach (built-in vs. tax service like TaxJar)
- Currency conversion strategy (real-time vs. fixed rates)
