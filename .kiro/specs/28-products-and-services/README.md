## Overview

Unified module that consolidates everything a business offers into one catalog: bookable services, physical merchandise, recurring memberships, pre-paid packages/bundles, and loyalty rewards. Orders pull from this catalog and feed into invoicing and payments.

**Core Features**:
- Services with variants, staff/resource/location requirements
- Merchandise (physical products, no inventory tracking)
- Memberships as recurring subscriptions with benefits
- Packages/bundles (pre-paid, redeemable over time)
- Loyalty/rewards programs (earn benefits from purchase history)
- Flexible pricing rules across all product types
- Unified orders combining any mix of items (including walk-in/anonymous)
