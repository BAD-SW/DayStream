# Phase 21: AI Features

## Status: 🔲 Not Started

## Objective
Differentiate the platform with AI-powered booking assistance, customer engagement, and business intelligence.

## Dependencies
- Phase 07: Booking Engine
- Phase 05: Customer Management
- Phase 17: Reporting & Analytics

## Scope Summary
- AI Booking Assistant:
  - Service recommendations based on customer goals
  - Natural language scheduling ("When can I book a massage this week?")
  - Recovery plan builder
  - Smart rebooking suggestions
- AI Receptionist (Chatbot):
  - Website chatbot for booking and FAQs
  - WhatsApp conversational booking
  - Membership advisor (recommend plans based on usage)
  - Multi-language support
- AI Business Assistant:
  - Revenue trend analysis and forecasting
  - Churn prediction (at-risk customers)
  - Staffing optimization recommendations
  - Demand-based pricing suggestions
  - Anomaly detection (unusual patterns)
- Personalization engine:
  - Recommended services per customer
  - Optimal booking time suggestions
  - Engagement scoring

## Key Decisions Pending
- LLM provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, self-hosted)
- AI hosting (API-based vs. edge inference)
- Training data strategy (per-tenant isolation)
- Chatbot platform (custom vs. Intercom/Drift with AI layer)
