# Phase 16: Marketing & Automation

## Status: 🔲 Not Started

## Objective
Build marketing tools for customer engagement — email, SMS, push notifications, and automated sequences.

## Dependencies
- Phase 05: Customer Management (segmentation)
- Phase 03: Core Platform

## Scope Summary
- Campaign types:
  - Email campaigns
  - SMS campaigns
  - Push notifications
- Customer segmentation targeting
- Automated sequences/workflows:
  - New member welcome
  - No visit in 30 days (re-engagement)
  - Membership expiring (renewal)
  - Birthday offers
  - Post-visit feedback
  - Abandoned booking
- Template builder (email, SMS)
- Campaign scheduling
- A/B testing
- Campaign analytics (open rate, click rate, conversions)
- Unsubscribe management
- Lead funnels (landing pages → conversion tracking)

## Key Decisions Pending
- Email delivery provider (SendGrid, Postmark, Resend, SES)
- SMS provider (Twilio, MessageBird)
- Template editor approach (drag-and-drop vs. code-based)
- Automation workflow builder UI
