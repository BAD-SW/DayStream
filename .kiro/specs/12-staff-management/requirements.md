# Phase 12: Staff Management

## Status: 🔲 Not Started

## Objective
Build staff profiles, scheduling, availability management, and permission system.

## Dependencies
- Phase 03: Core Platform
- Phase 02: Security & Compliance (roles/permissions)

## Scope Summary
- Staff profiles (name, photo, bio, qualifications, certifications, languages)
- Staff scheduling (weekly availability patterns)
- Holiday and leave management (sick, vacation, personal)
- Capacity management (max bookings per day/week)
- Service assignment (which staff deliver which services)
- Location assignment (which staff work at which locations)
- Staff permissions and roles
- Staff notifications (new bookings, schedule changes, cancellations)
- Performance tracking (sessions delivered, revenue generated)
- Staff onboarding workflow
- Public staff directory (customer-facing bios)

## Key Decisions Pending
- Scheduling UI approach (drag-and-drop calendar vs. form-based)
- Leave approval workflow (self-service vs. manager-approved)
- Staff self-service portal scope
